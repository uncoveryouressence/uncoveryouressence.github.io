Our Foundation

About Us

Uncover Your Essence (UYE) is a non-profit organization based in Nova Scotia, dedicated to equipping and empowering individuals, families, and communities to confidently embrace their identity, uncover their potential, and lead with purpose.

Our mission is to foster environments and initiatives that promote personal growth, self-care, and self-love, thereby boosting self-confidence and self-esteem among individuals and communities. We believe that by nurturing these qualities, we can inspire leadership and purposeful living.

Our Story

Our organization grew from the experiences and community work of founder Tiffani Young. Running self-care workshops in her business, she saw how many youth were struggling with challenges she had navigated herself, and set out to do something about it.

That vision became Uncover Your Essence Society, focused on closing gaps in representation, exposure, and access to resources that many young people face.

UYE’s first initiative was the 2025 Uncover Your Essence Conference for youth 13-18. After a successful inaugural year, it returned for a second time, continuing to empower youth through connection, education, representation, and opportunity.

A Transformative Day

Conference Overview

The second annual Uncover Your Essence (UYE) Conference, held on April 20, 2026, at the Halifax Convention Centre, brought together more than 1,500 youth, chaperones, speakers, performers, and community leaders from across Nova Scotia for a transformative day of empowerment and connection.

Targeting youth aged 13-18, the conference offered a full-day immersive experience centered around self-esteem, leadership development, cultural identity, and career readiness. The event featured a dynamic mix of breakout sessions, panel discussions, access to 29 interactive booths within the Fun & Knowledge Hub, and our newly introduced Discovery Zone.

What made UYE 2026 especially impactful was its continued intentionality for provincial representation. Every speaker, host, and participating organization is based in Nova Scotia.

Seven Pillars

Conference Objectives

The Uncover Your Essence (UYE) Conference 2026 was designed with the following key objectives. Click each to learn more.

Create a supportive environment where youth recognize their inherent worth, talents, and leadership potential.
Provide youth with tools, mentorship, and exposure to leadership pathways that equip them to lead confidently in their homes, schools, and communities.
Celebrate the diverse identities of Nova Scotian youth while fostering pride in cultural heritage and community connections.
Connect youth to real-world opportunities in education, trades, entrepreneurship, arts, STEM, and other career pathways through workshops, speakers, and the Fun & Knowledge Station.
Provide dedicated workshops and resources focused on building resilience, overcoming self-doubt, and promoting overall well-being.
Remove barriers to participation by covering transportation, meals, and event registration at no cost, with a focus on including youth from rural, marginalized, and underserved communities.
Develop new initiatives informed by youth voices and continued post-event follow-up to encourage ongoing youth involvement, innovation, and leadership development.

New in 2026

Discovery Zone

The Discovery Zone was a vibrant, interactive hub that brought creativity, skill, and storytelling to life through live demonstrations and real-time audience engagement. Designed to move beyond traditional booths, this space allowed youth to experience careers and creative pathways in action.

Live Demo

Barbering Showcase

Nathaniel Johnson, a barber, showcased his craft while sharing his personal journey in the industry. This activation gave attendees insight into the discipline, entrepreneurship, and creativity involved in barbering, while highlighting it as a viable and rewarding career path.

Live Music

Beat-Making Session

KekeBeatz, a music producer, led a live beat-making session alongside an artist, collaboratively creating a song in real time. Youth contributed ideas, sounds, direction, and vocals, becoming part of the creative process and seeing themselves as creators.

Inclusion By Design

Equity & Accessibility

Diversity, Equity, Inclusion and Accessibility were top of mind when designing and executing this conference. Youth from marginalized and underserved communities represented over 70% of attendees.

The event provided free transportation, accessible buses, accommodation, meals, a quiet room, an accessible venue, speakers and volunteers that represent various communities.

Looking Ahead

Next Steps

Ongoing Engagement
Follow-up outreach and engagement activities will continue through June 2026, providing additional opportunities for youth to remain connected and for feedback collection.
Post-Event Evaluation
A detailed survey was distributed throughout the conference and will continue to participating youth, schools, and chaperones during the week of May 3, 2026, focusing on overall satisfaction, knowledge gained, skills developed, and areas for future programming.
2026-2027 Planning
Learning from the UYE 2026 Conference will directly inform the structure, partnerships, and programming for UYE's expansion across the region, including rural Nova Scotia and New Brunswick, as well as the development of UYE 2027.
